## Short description

3DCC is an experimental spatial interface for navigating human perception. Instead of placing the observer outside a map, 3DCC places the observer at the coordinate origin: **@ = YOU**. Three perceptual axes divide the surrounding space into 27 cognitive zones, allowing objects to exist as positions, distances, directions and relationships around the viewer.

The first implementation maps cocktails. The user can rotate the field, explore its zones, enter **@**, and experience the coordinate from inside. From the origin, selecting an object creates a luminous vector from the observer toward it — turning selection into a visible cognitive reach.

## FWA description

Most interfaces place information in front of us: lists, tables, charts and maps. The observer remains outside the system.

3DCC changes the point of view.

The observer becomes the origin.

Built with Three.js, the experience organizes perceptual information across three dimensions. The first working implementation uses cocktails, distributed through 27 zones defined by Structure, Mouthfeel and Sweetness. But cocktails are not the destination; they are the first demonstration of a category-independent cognitive coordinate.

At the center is **@**. It is not another data point. It represents the viewer. Entering @ changes the experience from looking at a coordinate to occupying it. From inside, selecting a sphere sends a luminous vector from the observer toward the object.

The interface makes a simple proposition tangible: perception does not begin in the dataset. It begins with the observer.

**You are not looking at the map. You are inside it.**

---

## Why it matters

3DCC explores Three.js not as decoration, but as a functional interface for human perception.

A list expresses order. A field can express proximity, distance, direction, neighborhood, opposition and relationship to the observer.

The project asks whether information can be understood not only by reading or viewing it, but by occupying its coordinate system.

---

## Interaction highlights

1. **The field** — Rotate and explore a three-dimensional perceptual coordinate.
2. **27 cognitive zones** — Three axes × three levels create a navigable 3×3×3 structure.
3. **@ = YOU** — The observer is represented at the coordinate origin.
4. **Enter @** — Move from an external map view to a first-person view from the origin.
5. **Reach** — Select an object and a luminous vector connects the observer to it.
6. **Take Off** — Leave the first-person coordinate and return to the external field.
